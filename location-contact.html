How to Find the Masjid

  • Travel to Chak No. 77 RB in Khurrianwala, Faisalabad.
  • Look for Govt. Boys High School; the masjid is next to it.
  • The masjid is in front of Dera Ch. Mola Baksh Gujjar.
  • Or use the map above or the Google Maps link to get turn-by-turn directions from where you are.

Planning Your Visit

Jamaat times are Fajr 6:05 AM, Zuhr 1:30 PM, Asr 4:00 PM and Isha 7:00 PM. Maghrib Jamaat is 4 minutes after the sunset Azan, so arrive before sunset. See today's prayer timings for the live times and the full year's Maghrib Azan. To meet the Imam and Muazzin, see Imam & Muazzin.
